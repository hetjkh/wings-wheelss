# Destination additions

The destination guides use the same framework for Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Dubai & UAE, Maldives and Europe. These are planning ideas, not packages or guaranteed services, flight routes or travel durations.

Tourism sources reviewed on 2026-10-17:

- Kenya: https://magicalkenya.com/ and https://tic.magicalkenya.com/circuits/
- Tanzania: https://tanzaniatourism.go.tz/ and Tanzania National Parks destination material at https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/
- Uganda: https://www.tourism.go.ug/ and https://ugandawildlife.org/national-parks/bwindi-impenetrable-national-park/
- UAE: https://www.visitdubai.com/en/plan-your-trip
- Maldives: https://visitmaldives.com/en
- Europe: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/index_en.htm

Trip lengths are illustrative editorial starting points. Entry eligibility, processing times, fares, airline timetables and health requirements are not hard-coded. Destination essentials link to an official source and ask travellers to confirm requirements for their passport and itinerary.

The departure board represents journeys the team can coordinate, not a claim of direct flights. The destination enquiry form prepares a reviewable brief, then hands it to WhatsApp or the user's email client only when the user selects that action. It does not display a false submission confirmation or store personal information.
