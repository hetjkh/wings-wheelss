## Desktop prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: high-resolution full-screen background photograph for a premium travel website closing section.
Primary request: A completely new 'quiet moment before departure' visual concept, replacing generic mountain clouds. Generate a maximum-detail 3840x2160 landscape image (16:9).
Scene: An exceptionally elegant modern airport departure lounge on a Mediterranean coast, photographed from inside through immense unobstructed floor-to-ceiling curved glass. A single realistic commercial passenger aircraft sits outside on the apron in the middle distance, oriented to the right; beyond it luminous blue sea and a distant rugged coastline. Warm sunrise just outside frame.
Composition: Architectural editorial photograph, expansive panoramic composition, restrained sculptural limestone arch and dark bronze frame at far left and right edges, richly textured pale travertine floor in foreground, simple low leather seats confined to far bottom corners. The airport apron and aircraft are visible above the vertical midpoint. Large quiet darker wall/glass areas in upper left and upper right for white website headings; uncluttered lower central region for an HTML boarding-pass overlay. No actual UI in image.
Photography: lifelike premium architectural photography, extremely crisp glass, convincing aircraft proportions and landing gear, accurate perspective, refined natural color and soft realistic shadows, rich tactile materials, realistic dynamic range, no artificial HDR halos, no blur across image.
Palette: warm champagne light and stone, deep bronze shadows, restrained clear blue sea.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no interface, no split panels, no collage, no mountains above clouds, no people.
Return a production-quality high-resolution bitmap and save the generated file.
